Climatic actions

Snow loads (EN 1991-1-3) and wind actions (EN 1991-1-4) on a building with a rectangular base, given zone by zone and case by case.

Climatic actions depend on the National Annex in force at the construction site: the active profile and the date of its data are always shown, to be checked against the text applicable to the project. vb,0 and sk are always entered.

Outputs

The CSV has one line per zone and per case. The JSON has the characteristic actions, without combination or partial factor. The note opens in a tab and prints to PDF from the browser.

Out of scope: hipped, multispan and vaulted roofs; wind zoning of pitched roofs (Tables 7.3 and 7.4, next batch); non-rectangular or slender buildings (h/d > 5); dynamic analysis; exceptional snow; calculation of co by Annex A.3.
